1. Introduction

SkillSwap is a peer-to-peer learning platform that helps users connect with other people to exchange knowledge, practice skills and learn together.

By creating an account or using SkillSwap, you agree to follow these Terms & Conditions.

2. Your Account

You are responsible for providing accurate information when creating your SkillSwap account and for keeping your login credentials secure.

  • Do not create an account using another person's identity.
  • Do not share your password with other people.
  • Keep your profile information reasonably accurate and up to date.
  • Notify SkillSwap if you believe your account has been accessed without permission.

3. Skills & Assessments

Users may select skills they can teach and skills they want to learn.

Skill levels displayed by SkillSwap are based on assessments and learning progress. Users should not intentionally misrepresent their abilities or experience.

Skill levels are earned.

Selecting a skill during profile setup does not automatically grant Beginner, Intermediate or Pro status.

4. Skill Exchange

SkillSwap is designed around respectful, peer-to-peer learning relationships.

When connecting with another user, both users are responsible for communicating clearly about their learning goals, availability and expectations.

  • Respect your learning partner.
  • Do not intentionally waste another user's time.
  • Do not falsely claim expertise.
  • Do not pressure another user into an activity they do not want to participate in.
  • Keep communication relevant to the learning relationship.

5. Sessions & Scheduling

Users may schedule sessions with an accepted SkillSwap connection.

Session participants should make reasonable efforts to attend sessions they have confirmed. If plans change, users should communicate with their partner and cancel or reschedule where appropriate.

SkillSwap does not guarantee that another user will attend, teach a particular skill or achieve a particular learning outcome.

6. Learning Content & Submissions

SkillSwap may provide explanations, examples, exercises, roadmaps and practical tasks.

Users are responsible for the content they submit through practical tasks, profiles, messages and reviews.

Do not submit content that violates another person's rights or applicable laws.

7. Communication

SkillSwap may provide messaging tools so connected users can discuss sessions and learning goals.

Users must not use these communication features for harassment, threats, spam, scams or other abusive behaviour.

8. Reviews & Ratings

Users may leave reviews after eligible completed sessions.

Reviews should reflect the user's genuine experience and should remain respectful and constructive.

Fake reviews, manipulated ratings, harassment or intentionally misleading feedback are not allowed.

9. Paid Teaching & Mentoring

SkillSwap may offer optional paid teaching or mentoring features for eligible users.

Any applicable pricing, payment, cancellation, refund and service conditions will be displayed separately when such features are available.

SkillSwap remains free.

A user does not need to charge for teaching simply because they have earned a Pro level. Paid teaching is an optional feature.

You must not use SkillSwap to:

  • Harass, threaten or abuse another user.
  • Impersonate another person.

  • Attempt to gain unauthorized access to another user's account.

  • Circumvent platform restrictions or security mechanisms.

11. User Safety

SkillSwap is a platform for connecting people. Users should exercise appropriate judgment when interacting with other users.

Do not share passwords, financial credentials, security codes or other sensitive information with another user.
