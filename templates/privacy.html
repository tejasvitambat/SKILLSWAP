Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. This page explains what information SkillSwap may collect, why it is used, and how it is handled.

Last updated: September 2026
01

1. Introduction

SkillSwap is a peer-to-peer learning platform designed to help people teach, learn and exchange skills with one another.

This Privacy Policy describes the types of information that may be collected when you use SkillSwap and how that information may be used to operate and improve the platform.

02

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, SkillSwap may collect information such as:

  • Name and username
  • Email address
  • Profile photo, if you choose to upload one
  • Profile biography and location information
  • Skills you can teach
  • Skills you want to learn
  • Skill assessment results and earned levels
  • Learning roadmap and progress information
  • Session and connection information
  • Reviews and ratings you submit
  • Messages and other content you voluntarily submit through the platform
03

3. Account Information

Information provided during signup and profile setup may be used to create and maintain your SkillSwap account.

Your username, profile information, selected skills and earned skill levels may be displayed to other users when necessary for the platform's matching and learning features.

04

4. Skills & Assessment Data

SkillSwap may store information about the skills you select, assessments you complete, scores and earned levels.

✓
Your skill level is earned through assessment.

Assessment results may be used to determine your displayed level and improve skill matching.

05

5. Matching & Connections

SkillSwap may use your selected teach and learn skills, earned levels and relevant profile preferences to identify potential learning partners.

Information relevant to a potential match may be shown to another user so that both users can decide whether they want to connect.

06

6. Messages & Communication

When you use SkillSwap messaging features, messages may be stored so that they can be delivered and displayed to the participants of the relevant conversation.

You should avoid sharing passwords, payment credentials or other highly sensitive information through ordinary SkillSwap messages.

07

7. Sessions & Learning Progress

SkillSwap may store information about scheduled, completed or cancelled sessions in order to provide session management and learning progress features.

Completed topics, practical tasks and roadmap progress may also be associated with your account.

08

8. Reviews & Ratings

Reviews and ratings submitted after eligible sessions may be displayed on relevant profiles or within other SkillSwap features.

Before submitting a review, make sure that it does not contain private information that you do not want to share with other users.

09

9. How Information May Be Used

Information collected by SkillSwap may be used to:

  • Create and maintain your account
  • Provide profile and skill-matching features
  • Conduct and record skill assessments
  • Provide learning roadmaps and progress tracking
  • Facilitate connections and communication
  • Manage sessions and scheduling
  • Provide certificates and achievement records
  • Display reviews and ratings
  • Improve platform functionality and user experience
  • Maintain platform security and prevent abuse
10

10. Cookies & Similar Technologies

SkillSwap may use cookies or similar technologies that are necessary for features such as authentication, sessions, preferences and platform functionality.

Additional analytics or optional technologies may be introduced as the platform evolves.

11

11. Security

SkillSwap should use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect account information from unauthorized access, alteration or misuse.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

12

12. Sharing of Information

SkillSwap may display certain profile and learning information to other users when required for features such as matching, connections, sessions and reviews.

Information may also be processed by service providers when necessary to operate the platform, subject to applicable agreements and requirements.

🔒
We do not make your private credentials public.

Passwords and authentication credentials should never be displayed as part of your public profile.

13

13. Public Profile Information

Depending on your profile settings and the features being used, other SkillSwap users may be able to see information such as:

  • Your name or username
  • Profile photo
  • Biography
  • Skills you teach
  • Skills you want to learn
  • Earned skill levels
  • Relevant ratings and reviews
  • Certificates or achievements
14

14. Your Responsibility

You are responsible for the information you choose to add to your SkillSwap profile and the content you voluntarily share with other users.

Do not publish information that you do not have the right to share.

15

15. Data Retention

Account and learning information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the requested services, maintain records, protect the platform and comply with applicable obligations.

16

16. Account Deletion

SkillSwap may provide account-management features that allow users to request deletion or closure of their account.

Certain information may need to be retained where necessary for legitimate operational, security or legal purposes.

17

17. Children & Age Requirements

SkillSwap should only be used by people who meet the minimum age requirements applicable to the service and their location.

If you believe that a minor has provided personal information improperly, contact the SkillSwap support team.

18

18. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when SkillSwap introduces new features, changes its practices or when required by applicable requirements.

The latest version will be made available through the SkillSwap platform.

19

19. Contact

If you have questions or concerns about privacy, contact the SkillSwap support team through the support channel provided by the platform.
